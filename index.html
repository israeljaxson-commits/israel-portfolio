The approach

Generative video fails when every shot looks like a screensaver. I treat AI clips as raw dailies—cutting on action, matching grain and color in Resolve, and building the emotion in the sound mix.

Generative tools can produce endless frames, but cinema lives in restraint—narrative tension, lens consistency, rhythmic cuts, and atmospheric sound. Every piece is crafted like a short film first.
